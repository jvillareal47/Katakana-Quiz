Welcome Students!

Japanese uses Katakana to write Gairaigo (words borrowed from other languages, mainly English). Foreign sounds adapt to Japanese moraic structure where consonant clusters receive trailing vowels (e.g., "Toilet" becomes Toiretto トイレット).
